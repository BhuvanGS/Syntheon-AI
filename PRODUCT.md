# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and engineering leads who drown in meetings and still have to turn talk into tickets, boards, and shipped work. They open the marketing site mid-chaos: too many calls, no clean handoff to execution. They need to feel, in seconds, that Syntheon Hub closes that gap.

## Product Purpose

Syntheon Hub joins meetings, extracts structured tickets, and lands them on a board with dependencies and sprint context — so talk becomes work without manual project-management theater. Success for the brand surface: a founder or eng lead feels “this is inevitable” and starts a free trial.

## Brand Personality

**Talk · Work · Done**

- Voice: precise, calm, inevitable — never hype-y agent marketing.
- Emotion: kinetic intelligence under dark precision; quiet confidence that the system finishes what the meeting started.
- Motion language: Apple-like — scroll storytelling, expo ease-outs, intentional reveals; never bounce or decorative noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic “AI agent” landing pages (purple glow, stock photos of robots/people at laptops, identical icon+card feature grids).
- Anything that reads as template-generated or “referenced some images.”
- Busy dashboard collages, neon gradients, glassmorphism for its own sake.
- Soft SaaS cream / terracotta editorial defaults.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the transformation** — Talk → Work → Done is the narrative spine; UI demos beat adjectives.
2. **Premium finish over novelty** — hairline borders, refined radii, disciplined type; one signature motion moment, not a carnival.
3. **Dark precision** — near-black canvas, crystalline contrast, sparse sections with breathing room.
4. **Founder clarity** — one hero job per viewport; no stats-strip clutter in the first screen.
5. **Practice what you ship** — motion and layout feel as considered as the product claims to be.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support: crossfades or static states instead of scroll choreography.
- Keyboard-visible focus; no information conveyed by color alone.
- Body line length capped; mobile-first responsiveness without horizontal overflow.
